I am a Senior Machine Learning Engineer at PROPHESEE in Paris, working on machine learning and computer vision for event-based cameras.
I completed a PhD at KU Leuven in Hybrid Probabilistic Logic Programming.
My research background includes probabilistic programming, Bayesian inference, planning, robotics, machine learning, reinforcement learning and computer vision.
